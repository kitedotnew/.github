## Kite

Kite is the design tool your AI agents work in. Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and Codex draw banners, social posts, ads, logos and screens into a design file, and your team edits and comments on the same file live, in your own design system.

**[Start designing at kite.new](https://kite.new)**. It's free to start.

### Connect your agent

- **Claude**: one click from the [connector directory](https://claude.ai/directory/connectors/kite)
- **ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex and other agents**: [connect guides](https://kite.new/docs/agents)
- **Any MCP client**: `https://kite.new/api/mcp` ([docs](https://kite.new/docs/mcp))
- **Mac app**: [download](https://kite.new/download)
